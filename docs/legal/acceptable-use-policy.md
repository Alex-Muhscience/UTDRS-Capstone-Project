# UTDRS acceptable use policy

> **Draft for legal review. Not legal advice.**

Last updated: `[DATE]`

This policy applies to everyone who uses UTDRS. It forms part of the
[terms of service](terms-of-service.md).

## Use UTDRS to defend what you are responsible for

Connect only services, accounts and devices your organisation owns or is authorised to
monitor, and monitor people only as the law and your own policies allow.

## Do not

- Use UTDRS to monitor people or organisations you have no right to monitor, or to stalk,
  harass or surveil anyone.
- Use response actions to disrupt accounts or services you are not responsible for.
- Try to reach another organisation's data, or get round UTDRS's separation between
  organisations, its authentication or its limits.
- Probe, scan or test UTDRS's security without our written permission. Report suspected
  vulnerabilities to `[SECURITY CONTACT]`; we will not pursue good-faith research that
  follows that process.
- Overload UTDRS (beyond its documented limits) or use it to send unsolicited email.
- Upload malware to UTDRS except as evidence attached to a case, where it is kept and only
  ever downloaded, never run.
- Resell or share access with people outside your organisation without our agreement.

## What happens if this policy is broken

We may suspend the access involved, and, where the breach is serious or repeated, end the
subscription, as the terms of service allow. We will tell you why unless the law prevents it.
