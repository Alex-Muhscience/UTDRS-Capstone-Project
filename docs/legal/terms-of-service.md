## 1. The service

UTDRS is a threat detection and response service. It receives security events from the
services you connect (such as Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace) and from sources you send
events from, analyses them for threats, groups alerts into cases, notifies the people you
choose, and, if you set it to, takes response actions in your connected services (such as
signing a user out, suspending an account or removing an email).

We provide the service as described in its documentation, which may change as the service
develops. We will not materially reduce the core detection and response functions during a
paid subscription term without telling you `[30]` days in advance.

## 3. Connected services and response actions

- When you connect a service, you authorise UTDRS to read from it and, for response actions,
  to act in it, within the permissions you grant there. You may disconnect at any time.
- **You choose how UTDRS responds**: only suggest, ask before acting, or act on its own within
  safety limits. Actions taken automatically follow the playbooks and limits you set.
  Response actions can affect your people's ability to work. Before choosing automatic
  response, you should review the playbooks and protected accounts.
- UTDRS refuses certain actions to avoid lockouts (for example disabling an organisation's
  last administrator), but you remain responsible for your configuration of connected services.
